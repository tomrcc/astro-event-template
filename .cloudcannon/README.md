# Signal & Noise website

This site is the home of the conference: the programme, speakers, tickets and contact details.

## Quick links

- [Pages](cloudcannon:collections/pages)
- [Speakers](cloudcannon:collections/speakers)
- [Site settings](cloudcannon:collections/data)

## Pages

Most pages are built from sections. Open a page in the Visual Editor, click any text to change it, and use the section controls to add, move or remove sections.

To make a new page, click **+ Add** in Pages:

- **Page builder** for a page made of sections. Until it's saved and built, a new page shows the Contact page as its preview.
- **Text page** for a simple page of text, such as a policy.

New pages aren't added to the navigation automatically. Add a link under **Site settings > site > Navigation**.

## Speakers

Each speaker has their own page. Add a speaker here first, then choose them in a page's **Speakers** or **Schedule** section.

## Site settings

- **site**: the site name, navigation, header button and footer.
- **event**: the event's name, date and venue, shown in the hero and footer.
- **chat**: the chat assistant's greeting, suggested questions and extra instructions.

## Parts that come from the server

A few parts of the site only work on the live site. In the editor they show an example, marked with a purple icon. Hover over or click the icon to see what the live site shows instead.

- **Seats left** in the Tickets section shows an example number. You can change the wording around the number in the sidebar.
- **Chat assistant** answers from everything on the site, so keep the FAQ and pages up to date and it stays accurate. Sending is off in the editor.
- **Enquiry form** sends messages on to the team. Sending is off in the editor.
