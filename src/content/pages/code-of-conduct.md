---
title: Code of conduct
description: How we expect everyone at Signal & Noise to treat each other.
---

Signal & Noise is for everyone. We don't tolerate harassment of attendees, speakers, volunteers or staff in any form.

## What we expect

- Be kind, and assume good intent.
- Respect people's boundaries, names and pronouns.
- Look out for each other.

## Reporting

Find any volunteer in a yellow lanyard, or email conduct@example.com. Every report is handled in confidence.
